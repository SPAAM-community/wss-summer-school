## deNBI project request

This guide assumes you have already signed up to deNBI.

1. To request a new deNBI project, go to the [central portal](https://cloud.denbi.de/portal/).
2. In the side bar, go to the 'Project Management' section and click on 'New Project'.
3. Press 'New SimpleVM application'
4. In the form set the following information:
   - General Information
     - **Project Name**: `SPAAM Summer School 20XX` (where XX is the year of the summer school)
     - **Short Name**: `SPAAMSumSchoXX` (where XX is the year of the summer school)
     - **Project Description**:
       This project is for the XXXX SPAAM Summer School: Introduction to Ancient Metagenomics (https://spaam-community.github.io/wss-summer-school/). We successfully used the deNBI SimpleVM workshop in the previous three years for the first, second, and third edition of the summer school, and are happy to use it again. This years workshop will run from August Xth-Xth 20XX. The workshop aims to introduce to up to 40 advanced B.Sc/M.Sc./early Ph.D. students to the main steps of ancient metagenomic bioinformatic workflows, from familiarising students with the command line, to demonstrating how to process next-generation-sequencing (NGS) data, and to showing how to perform de novo metagenomic assembly and pylogenomic analysis. The course is designed specifically to be highly hands-on and practical, given the interdisiciplinary nature of our field. We therefore want to provide thorough introduction and training to researchers with little computational training. Examples of tools that will be explored include: - bash - git - conda - singularity or docker - nextflow - R (+ tidyverse packages) - python (+ pandas, seaborn) - kraken2 - bwa - samtools - megahit - humann2 - MEGAX - RAXML - BEAST2
       (where XX or XXX is the year of the summer school)
     - **Desired lifetime**: `4 months`
     - **Training**: `No` (or up to you)
     - **Workshop**: `Yes`
   - **Resources**
     - de.NBI medium (cores: 16, RAM 32GB): 50 (or as required, students + instructors)
     - Number of storage volumes: 50 (or as required, students + instructors)
     - Volume storage total limit: 4000 GB (or as required, students + instructors, e.g. 4000 GB / 50 VMs = ~80GB per VM)
   - Additional information:
     - **Institute Name**: Max Planck Institute for the Evolutionary Anthropology
     - **Workgroup Name**: Microbiome Science
     - **Research Topics**: metagenomics
     - **Person related data**:
       - Processes of human subjects not publicly available: `No`
       - The project will not process any data of human subjects: `Yes`
     - **Horizon Europe**: as required
     - **Elixir Project**: as required
     - **DFG**: as required
     - **BMBF**: as required
     - **NFDI**: as required
     - **Dissemination**: `Yes`
     - **Principal Investigator**: `No`
     - **Link to PI verification**: https://www.eva.mpg.de/archaeogenetics/research-groups/microbiome-sciences/
     - **Principal Investigator Email**: as relevant
5. Press submit
6. Tell PI to check their email and approve the project request.
7. Wait for deNBI VO to approve the project request.
