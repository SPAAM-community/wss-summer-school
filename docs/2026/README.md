# Introduction to Ancient Metagenomics Summer School - 2026

The Werner Siemens-Stiftung funded SPAAM Summer School will be **taking a break in 2026**, to complete the official publication of the companion open access Introduction to Ancient Metagenomics textbook!

In the meantime, you can still **go through the summer school material independently** by checking the [online textbook](https://www.spaam-community.org/intro-to-ancient-metagenomics-book/), or watch the previous years lectures on the SPAAM [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@spaam-community/playlists).

**Future editions of the summer school** will be announced on the usual channels, such as the [SPAAM mailing list and social media platforms](https://www.spaam-community.org/about/).
